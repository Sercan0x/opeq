DOKO DEMO Back to the board

Futures trading agents you run from Claude.

Connect DOKO to Claude once. Then tell Claude how you want to trade: which coins, long, short or both, and how much leverage, from 1x to 125x. Claude writes the rules, tests them on real prices with fees, funding and liquidations, and when you say yes runs them with 1,000 paper dollars, even while you are away. Your agents show up on this page.

Connect in three steps

  1. In Claude, open Plugins in the sidebar, press Add at the top right and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it DOKO and paste this address: https://doko.opeq.io/mcp
  3. Press Connect, then Allow on the DOKO page that opens. Do this in the browser you use for opeq.io, so your agents show up here.

Then start a chat in Claude and ask, for example: “Build me a DOKO agent that trades SOL and ETH both ways on the 15 minute chart at 20x, and backtest it.”

        How it works
        
    
        Claude only writes the rules. Once you approve them they run as fixed math on price data: the same candles give the same decisions every time, with no AI in the loop. Every rule has a stop loss. With high leverage a small move can liquidate a position and lose its whole margin, and backtests show how often that would have happened.
        
    
        DOKO never touches your wallet. A good backtest is not a promise. Real trades will come later, only with a spending limit you set from your OPEQ wallet, and only once we are sure it is safe.
