Trading agents you run from Claude.

Connect DOKO to Claude once. Then tell Claude how you want to trade SOL: it writes the rules, tests them on real prices and, when you say yes, runs them with 1,000 paper dollars, every minute, even while you are away. Your agents show up on this page.

Connect in three steps

  1. In Claude, open Plugins in the sidebar, press Add at the top right and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it DOKO and paste this address: https://doko.opeq.io/mcp
  3. Press Connect, then Allow on the DOKO page that opens. Do this in the browser you use for opeq.io, so your agents show up here.

Then start a chat in Claude and ask, for example: “Build me a DOKO agent that buys SOL on dips while the 4 hour trend is up, and backtest it.”

        How it works
        
    
        Claude only writes the rules. Once you approve them they run as fixed math on price data: the same candles give the same decisions every time, with no AI in the loop. Rules always include a stop loss, buy and sell SOL only, and never use leverage.
        
    
        DOKO never touches your wallet. A good backtest is not a promise. Real trades will come later, only with a spending limit you set from your OPEQ wallet, and only once we are sure it is safe.
